Privacy Policy

This policy explains the information collected when you use Topeka Water Heater Pros or send a service inquiry.

Request service

Share the details and we will follow up. Prefer to talk? Call (877) 761-0283.

Use this form for service inquiries, not emergencies. If you smell gas, leave the building and contact your gas utility or emergency services from a safe place.

Information you provide

The request form collects your name, phone number, optional email and a description of the problem. It also records the site, page and page code associated with the inquiry. Avoid putting payment details or other sensitive information in the message.

Request handling and site measurement

We use the information to respond to the inquiry and support service-request handling. The site uses an external form-tracking service and Google Analytics to process submission events and understand site use. These services may collect device, browser and usage information and use cookies or similar technology.

Questions about your information

Call (877) 761-0283 or use the contact page to ask about a request or the information you provided. This policy does not promise a fixed retention period or processing outcome.
