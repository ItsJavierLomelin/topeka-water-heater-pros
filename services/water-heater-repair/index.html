What changed before the hot water stopped?

A complete loss of hot water, a short supply and a problem at one tap are different starting points. Note when it began and whether the heater displays an error. A professional can check the appropriate heating components, controls and connections without guessing from the symptom alone.

What should you tell us about the heater?

The model label, fuel type and access route help explain the setup. In Potwin or College Hill, an older basement may have later alterations that are not apparent from the age of the house. Share what you know about previous heater changes, but do not move the unit or open electrical or burner covers.

A visible leak needs a closer look

Water beside the heater does not by itself show where the leak starts. Connections, the surrounding plumbing and the tank need to be distinguished. If water is near electrical equipment, keep away and get professional help. If you smell gas, leave the property and call your gas utility or emergency services from a safe location.

Common questions

Can a repair be confirmed from the form?

No. A description helps organize the inquiry, but the cause and repairability need to be assessed. Do not assume a part replacement will solve a leak in the tank itself.

What if only the upstairs shower goes cold?

Explain whether other fixtures still have hot water and whether the change happens while another tap is running. The shower valve, distribution and overall demand may need to be checked as well as the heater.
