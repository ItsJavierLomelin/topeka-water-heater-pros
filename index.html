Before you request water heater service

Is no hot water always a failed heater?

Not necessarily. A problem at one faucet may have a different cause from a whole-house loss of hot water. Describe which fixtures are affected and whether the heater has a visible error code. Do not remove covers or work on gas or electrical connections.

Can an older Topeka house use a new tank water heater?

Often it can, but the replacement must fit the space and its connections must be assessed. A heater in an older basement may need a different access plan from one in a utility closet. The existing tank size is not enough to settle the choice.

Should I switch to tankless when the tank needs replacing?

It depends on simultaneous hot water demand and the property. A gas tankless system may need different fuel supply and venting. Discuss the complete installation rather than choosing by cabinet size alone.

Does sending this form book a visit?

No. It starts a service inquiry. Availability, the job scope and scheduling are confirmed separately.
